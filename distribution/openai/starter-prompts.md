# OpenAI starter prompts

Status: draft copy for a Skills-only listing.

Official guidance asks for prompts that demonstrate high-value, realistic workflows while remaining adaptable. The first four below are the recommended initial listing set.

## Recommended listing prompts

### 1. Positioning and argument-led outline

Use these approved meeting notes, recent competing Reviews, and verified evidence cards to lock the article type, reader, governing question, source authority, architecture ownership or hierarchy rule, and defensible difference. Produce the requested one-page or detailed outline with section jobs, anchor evidence, display roles, and an author-side unresolved-decision log. Treat any old outline marked “format only” as formatting, not scientific authority.

### 2. Evidence-bounded author first draft

Draft the manuscript from my approved outline, verified literature, results, figures, protocols, and author notes. Write only the claims those sources support, keep provisional items in a separate author query log, merge all editorial passes into one authorial voice, and keep decision provenance, evidence states, prompts, TODOs, and internal labels outside the reader-visible draft.

### 3. Full manuscript revision

Revise this scientific manuscript into evidence-faithful submission English. Preserve every supported claim, number, citation, term, and project-defined scale; repair the central argument and paragraph logic; audit overclaiming, citations, figures, tables, captions, and cross-references; and return the revised manuscript with a concise risk log. If document rendering is available, verify the final pages.

### 4. Chinese-to-English reconstruction

Reconstruct this Chinese research draft as natural, field-appropriate English rather than translating sentence by sentence. Lock the scientific meaning, numbers, units, terminology, citations, and validation limits first, then provide the English revision and a short Chinese explanation of consequential decisions.

### 5. Skeptical peer review

Review this paper as a demanding domain expert and methods reviewer for the named journal or conference. Identify the strongest defensible contribution, unsupported claims, missing controls or baselines, alternative explanations, figure and table weaknesses, and likely reviewer objections. Separate issues that can be fixed in writing from those that require new evidence.

### 6. Claim-citation audit

Audit every consequential claim in this manuscript against its cited source. Check source existence and status, metadata, entailment, scope, placement, and independence; flag overclaiming or reused cohorts; and propose narrower wording when the evidence does not support the current sentence.

## Additional prompts

### 7. Scientific-title optimization

Read the supplied manuscript or abstract and propose concise, searchable English and Chinese scientific titles that match the article type, central contribution, evidence boundary, and target venue. Explain the trade-offs and reject wording that overstates novelty, mechanism, validation, or generality.

### 8. Rebuttal and synchronized revision

Prepare an evidence-faithful response to these reviewer comments using only the supplied manuscript, analyses, and new results. Map each response to the exact text, figure, table, or supplement change, preserve unresolved limitations, and do not claim that an unperformed experiment was completed.

### 9. Figure and table story

Audit the manuscript's figures, tables, captions, axes, units, uncertainty, sample sizes, statistical labels, provenance, accessibility, and cross-references. Recommend a clearer visual sequence that supports the central claim without hiding negative results or implying unsupported causality.

### 10. Review-method boundary

Determine whether this review draft is honestly a narrative review, structured narrative review, scoping review, or systematic review based on the documented search and screening record. Revise the methods and limitations to match what was actually done, and do not add PRISMA-style claims without a reproducible record.

## Prompt selection checks

- Use the plugin name naturally only when needed; prompts should also work through implicit routing.
- Keep manuscript work central. Identifier lookup, literature search, raw statistics, reference-style conversion, and generic translation alone are outside the skill's intended route.
- Do not include acceptance guarantees, publisher affiliation, AI-detector evasion, or invented evidence.
- If rendering, web lookup, or document tooling is unavailable in the host, the response must state that limitation rather than claim the check was completed.
