## Prohibited promotion tactics

- Purchased, botted, exchanged, or incentivized stars, forks, votes, comments, or testimonials.
- Engagement pods, vote rings, reciprocal-star groups, or asking colleagues to upvote a launch.
- Identical bulk posts across unrelated subreddits, forums, groups, or private messages.
- Fake user counts, success stories, institutional affiliations, directory approvals, or acceptance outcomes.
- Uploading a confidential manuscript or unredacted audit output as a public demo.
- Announcing a directory listing before the public entry is visible and installable.
