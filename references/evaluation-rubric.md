# CNS evaluation rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4. A full manuscript should not be called finished when a critical dimension is below 3.

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claim fidelity | claims changed or invented | core meaning mostly preserved, some drift | every consequential change traceable and scoped |
| Evidence alignment | citations absent/misleading | existence checked, entailment uneven | existence, entailment, scope, and placement checked |
| Argument architecture | topic list | sections coherent but synthesis uneven | governing question and decision logic are explicit |
| Paragraph function | fragmented or overloaded | mostly purposeful | each paragraph makes one clear intellectual move |
| Scholarly judgment | generic summary | occasional comparison | selection, comparison, and boundaries are explicit |
| Natural voice | formulaic or overprocessed | readable with recurrent templates | field-appropriate, varied, authorial, and precise |
| Accessibility | opaque to target reader | terminology mostly controlled | complexity is explained without flattening nuance |
| Artifact quality | broken/unreviewed | generated and spot-checked | every page, table, figure, and reference checked |

## Critical fail conditions

Any one of these blocks a journal-ready claim:

- fabricated or unverifiable citation presented as real;
- numerical, causal, novelty, or clinical claim with unresolved support;
- a conclusion stronger than the validation layer;
- unlabelled preprint/retraction/correction risk;
- broken figure/table/cross-reference;
- tracked changes or comments unintentionally left in the deliverable;
- a promise of AI-detector evasion or guaranteed “human score”.

## Reader-test questions

- Can the reader state the document's governing question after the introduction?
- Can the reader explain why the organizing axis is useful?
- Does each major comparison use the same dimensions?
- Are predictions, experiments, and deployment evidence visibly different?
- Can the reader locate the strongest unresolved assumption?
- Does the conclusion prescribe a more informative next experiment rather than generic future work?
